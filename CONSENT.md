# What Anton collects, and what you can do about it

**Version `2026-08-v1`.** If this document changes, we ask you again. The exact
text you agreed to is recorded against your record, so a later edit to this page
cannot change what you consented to.

This is written to be pasted into the creator WhatsApp community as-is.

---

## The short version

We record how your sponsored posts performed, so the brand paying for the
campaign can see the results. You send us a screenshot of your own Insights
panel. We read the numbers off it and keep the screenshot as proof the numbers
are real.

We never ask for your password. We never log into your account. We never install
anything on your phone.

---

## What we collect

**Things you tell us when you join**

- Your name and the handle you post under
- Which platform you post on (Instagram, TikTok)
- Your follower count at the moment you tell us, kept as dated entries so we
  never overwrite your history
- Your city and country, the languages you post in, and the topics you cover
- Your email address, if you give us one

**Things you send us during a campaign**

- The link to your post
- The date and time you posted
- **A screenshot of your own Insights panel**, which you take and upload
- The numbers we read from that screenshot: reach, impressions, likes,
  comments, shares, saves, profile visits, link clicks, video views, watch time,
  and follows from that post
- Your caption, and the opening line of your video, if you share them

**Things the system records automatically**

- When you opened your Anton link, and how many times
- When you gave consent, and which version of this document you agreed to
- A one-way scrambled version of your internet address at the moment you
  consented. This cannot be turned back into your address. We keep it only to
  show that consent genuinely happened.

## What we do not collect

- Your password, for anything
- Direct messages, comments on your posts, or anything from your inbox
- Your location beyond the city you tell us
- Your bank details. There is no payment system in this version of Anton at all.
- Anything at all from a post that is not part of a campaign you joined

---

## Why we collect it

**To show the brand what their campaign did.** This is the whole point. A brand
paying twenty creators wants to know what those twenty posts achieved.

**To prove the numbers are real.** Every figure we show a brand links back to
the screenshot it came from. If a brand questions a number, we can show them the
image. This is why we keep the screenshot rather than just the numbers.

**To pay you correctly, where a campaign pays a fee.**

**To know who to chase.** If you accepted a campaign and have not posted, the
system tells us so we can nudge you.

We do **not** sell your data. We do not share it with anyone other than the
brand running the campaign you joined. We do not use it to train any model.

---

## Who sees your screenshot

- **Anton staff**, when checking the numbers were read correctly.
- **The brand running that campaign**, if they open a report and click through
  to the source of a number.

Nobody else. Screenshots are stored privately. They are never on a public web
address. When someone with permission views one, the system generates a link
that stops working after a few minutes.

---

## How long we keep it

| What | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Your profile and handles | While you are in the roster, and 24 months after your last campaign |
| Post metrics | 24 months after the campaign ends |
| Screenshots | 24 months after the campaign ends |
| Consent record | 6 years, because we may have to prove consent existed |
| Your scrambled internet address | Deleted with the consent record |

After that we delete it. The consent record outlives the rest on purpose: it is
the evidence that we were allowed to hold the rest.

> **Note for Raghav, not for the community:** the 24-month figure and the 6-year
> consent retention are placeholders I chose as defensible defaults, not legal
> advice. Confirm both before this goes to real creators. The 6 years mirrors
> the standard UK contractual limitation period; 24 months is simply a round
> number that outlasts a typical campaign reporting cycle.

## What you can do, any time

Everything below is on your own Anton link. No email, no form, no waiting.

**See everything we hold on you.** One tap. Nothing hidden.

**Download it.** One tap, and you get a file with every record we hold about
you, including your screenshots.

**Delete it.** One tap. This removes your profile, your posts, your metrics and
your screenshots.

Two honest caveats about deletion:

1. **Reports already sent to a brand do not un-send.** If a brand downloaded a
   report last month, we cannot reach into their files. Going forward, your rows
   disappear from every report.
2. **We keep the consent record itself.** Not your data — just the dated note
   that you consented and then withdrew. Deleting the proof that we were allowed
   to hold your data would leave us unable to answer for having held it.

**Withdraw consent without deleting.** We stop using your data for anything new
but keep what is already there. Useful if you want out of future campaigns but
are happy for a finished campaign's report to stand.

**Change your mind again later.** Nothing here is one-way except deletion.

---

## Things worth being straight about

**The screenshot is the record.** We keep it because our promise to brands is
that every number traces back to something you actually showed us. If that is
not something you want kept, this is the point to say no. Joining is entirely
optional and nothing bad happens if you decline.

**We read the numbers with an AI model, and it is sometimes wrong.** A person
checks anything the model is unsure about before it reaches a brand. If you
think a number attributed to you is wrong, tell us and we will show you the
screenshot it came from.

**We cannot see anything you do not send us.** We have no access to your
account. If a campaign asks for a metric your screenshot does not show, we
record it as "not captured" rather than guessing.
